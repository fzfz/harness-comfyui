# 示例提示词（可直接复制使用）

以下 3 条为「动作迁移版」完整提示词，满足垂直全身 + 鞋履入镜 + 头≤25% + 腿≥65% 硬约束。复制进 ComfyUI（Krea2）即可出图，或作为图生视频的源人物参考图。

---

## 示例 1 · 国风仙侠（纯展示 / 动作迁移两用）

```
1girl, slender figure, long black hair with silver hairpin, purple eyes, delicate features.
Hanfu: floor-length lavender silk robe with cloud embroidery, wide sleeves, high waist.
Footwear: ivory flat silk slippers with cloud embroidery, fully visible at bottom of frame.
standing, full-body, feet together, arms at sides, looking at viewer, no extra motion.
Background: mountain lake at dawn, mist over water, pine-covered peaks, soft morning light.
anime style, cel shading, masterpiece, best quality, clean lineart.
```
